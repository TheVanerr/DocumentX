# 14.1 Annexes

This section defines the annexes to the manual and the form of delivery that applies to each annex. The annexes are an integral part of the manual.

| Annex | Content | Form of delivery |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | List of fault lamps | In this section |
| **B** | Process parameter record | Prepared by the operating company |
| **C** | Part-specific setting records | Prepared by the operating company |
| **D** | Warranty certificate | Separate document |
| **E** | Annual safety test and gas spring inspection record | In this section |

## 14.1.1 Annex A — List of fault lamps

The machine has no display that shows alarm codes; faults are indicated by the three red fault lamps on the control panel. The lamps are defined in **Chapter 3.4.10** and the diagnostic steps are given in **Chapter 11**.

| Lamp | Meaning | Machine behaviour | Diagnosis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Low water level** | Tank water level is below the safe operating limit | Heater and pump disabled; RESET required | Fault 3 |
| **Gear motor fault** | Thermal overload relay of the basket gear motor has tripped | Basket does not rotate; washing may not start | Fault 1 |
| **Pump fault** | Thermal overload relay of the washing pump has tripped | Pump does not run | Fault 2 |

Optional equipment such as the drying fan, the drain pump, the oil skimmer and the steam extraction fan has no fault lamp; the thermal protection of this equipment is checked inside the panel (Fault 4). When the residual current device (RCD) or the phase sequence relay trips, the machine does not run at all (**See Chapter 11.3**).

## 14.1.2 Annex B — Process parameter record

The process parameters are determined by the operating company; the manual does not give a fixed parameter table. The main setting points that must be recorded are as follows:

- Thermostat (GEMO DT481) target temperature (SV), maximum 70 °C,
- Timer (GEMO DZ482) washing time,
- Type and dosage of the washing chemical (according to the chemical manufacturer's safety data sheet and the application).

The parameter record must be kept in the operating company's document system (**See Chapter 6.7**). Customer parameter form reference: [EKSİK]

## 14.1.3 Annex C — Part-specific setting records

The type of part to be washed, the degree of soiling, the temperature, the time and the chemical concentration are determined by the operating company; the manufacturer does not provide a standard recipe example. The setting records by part type must be kept in accordance with the example table in **Chapter 8.2**. Customer setting record form reference: [EKSİK]

## 14.1.4 Annex D — Warranty certificate

The warranty certificate is delivered with the machine as a separate document and is not attached to this manual. The scope, period and conditions of the warranty apply only as stated in the warranty certificate (**See Chapter 13.1**).
