# 12. DISMANTLING AND DECOMMISSIONING

This chapter describes the dismantling of the machine for relocation to another site, its temporary or permanent decommissioning, and its scrapping at the end of its service life. These operations require the machine to be completely disconnected from its energy sources and the process water, oil and chemical waste it contains to be disposed of without harming the environment.

| Section | Topic |
| :--- | :--- |
| **12.1** | Dismantling |
| **12.2** | Temporary and permanent decommissioning |
| **12.3** | Scrapping and recycling |

Dismantling and scrapping must be carried out by authorised maintenance and electrical personnel. The electrical connection of the machine may be disconnected only by authorised electrical personnel.
