# 12.3 Scrapping and recycling

When the machine reaches the end of its service life, its components must be separated by material type and sent for recycling. Scrapping must be carried out in accordance with the waste management and waste electrical and electronic equipment legislation of the country in which the machine is used.

## 12.3.1 Separation of hazardous substances

Before scrapping, remove the following substances from the machine and dispose of them separately:

| Substance | Location | Disposal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Process water and chemical residue** | Tank, pump, pipes | In accordance with waste water legislation (**See Chapter 10.1.7**) |
| **Oil and sludge** | Tank bottom, filters, oil skimmer (option) | As oily waste, to a licensed waste contractor |
| **Gearbox oil** | Basket gear motor | As waste oil, to a licensed waste contractor |
| **Chemical packaging** | – | In accordance with the chemical manufacturer's instructions |

## 12.3.2 Separation of materials

| Material group | Components | Recycling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stainless steel** | Body, washing chamber, tank, lid, piping, filters | Scrap metal |
| **Galvanised steel** | Basket (galvanised type), transport trolley (option) | Scrap metal |
| **Electrical and electronic equipment** | Electrical panel and its internal components, thermostat, timer, switches, sensors, cables | Waste electrical and electronic equipment collection point |
| **Motors** | Washing pump, basket gear motor, option motors | Electrical equipment / metal recycling |
| **Heaters** | Heating element assemblies | Scrap metal |
| **Plastic and rubber** | Hoses, seals, cable ducts | Plastic waste |
| **Gas springs** | Lid gas springs | Contain pressurised gas; disposed of in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions |

**WARNING — Pressurised gas:** The lid gas springs contain pressurised gas. Drilling, cutting or heating a gas spring can cause a sudden release of gas and flying parts. Support the lid when removing the gas springs; dispose of the gas springs separately without breaking them up.
