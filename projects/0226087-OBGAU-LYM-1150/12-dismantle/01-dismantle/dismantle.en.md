# 12.1 Dismantling

As the machine is shipped as a single unit, it does not need to be broken down into parts for relocation to another site. Dismantling covers disconnecting the machine from the site connections and preparing it for transport.

## 12.1.1 Prerequisites for dismantling

- The machine must have cooled down after its last use.
- The waste water collection point into which the tank water will be drained must be ready.
- The forklift that will move the machine and an authorised forklift operator must be ready (**See Chapter 4.1.3**).

## 12.1.2 Dismantling sequence

1. Stop the machine with the STOP button; turn the heater switch, the oil skimmer switch (if fitted) and the main switch to the **0** position.
2. Apply LOTO (**See Chapter 2.4**).
3. Wait for the process water to cool down to a safe temperature.
4. Drain the tank water through the drain valve or with the drain pump and dispose of the waste water in accordance with **Chapter 10.1.7**.
5. Clean and dry the tank, the filters and the washing chamber (**See Chapter 10.1.4**).
6. Unplug the supply plug from the socket; coil the cable and secure it by wrapping it around the steam vent outlet on the right-hand side surface.
7. Disconnect the drain line.
8. Disconnect the external connections of the optional equipment (steam vent duct, drain pump outlet line, etc.).
9. On machines with a transport trolley, separate the trolley from the machine.
10. On machines with an interlock lock, set the lock to the **UNLOCK** position so that the lid can be opened during transport (**See Chapter 5.4.1**).
11. Move the machine with a forklift in accordance with **Chapter 4.1.3**.

If the machine is to be installed at a new site, carry out all of the installation and commissioning steps in **Chapter 5** again.
