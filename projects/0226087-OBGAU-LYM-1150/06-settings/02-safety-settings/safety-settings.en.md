# 6.2 Safety-related settings

The safety functions of the machine consist of the emergency stop, the reset, the lid-closed switch, the low water level protection, the motor thermal protection and, where fitted, the interlock lock. None of these functions has a setting that can be made by the user.

| Parameter | Value / status |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Safety door or lid switch bypass** | None; not bypassed for maintenance either |
| **Light curtain** | None |
| **Interlock lock (option)** | Set to the **LOCK** position during installation (**See Chapter 5.4.1**) |
| **Process water temperature limit** | 70 °C (factory setting, cannot be changed) |
| **Emergency stop function test interval** | Once a year (**See Chapter 5.4.2, 9.1.8**) |
| **Electrical safety tests (LVD)** | Once a year (**See Chapter 9.1.8**) |
| **Lid gas springs** | Monthly check; replacement on loss of force (**See Chapter 9.1.7**) |

**WARNING — Disabled protection:** Bridging the lid-closed switch, the interlock lock or the emergency stop circuit can lead to hot water being sprayed while the lid is open, to hands being caught by the rotating basket and to scalding. Never bridge or disable the safety devices under any circumstances. When a faulty safety device is detected, take the machine out of service and replace the device (**See Chapter 11**).
