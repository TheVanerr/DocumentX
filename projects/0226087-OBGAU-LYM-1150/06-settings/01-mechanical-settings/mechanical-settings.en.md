# 6.1 Mechanical settings

The machine has no mechanical settings to be made by the user. The basket drive system, the spray arms, the nozzles and the lid gas springs have been fitted and adjusted at the factory.

| Setting point | Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reference / home position** | None |
| **Chain or belt tension** | None (the basket is driven directly by the gear motor) |
| **Limit switch distance adjustment** | None |
| **Nozzle position** | Fixed; requires no adjustment |
| **Format / product changeover** | None; no change of settings is required for different parts |

Changing the nozzle type (standard 0° nozzle or flat spray nozzle) is a part replacement, not an adjustment. A change of nozzle type may only be made with the approval of the manufacturer (**See Chapter 3.1.3**).
