# 6.7 Process parameters

The process parameters are determined by the operating company according to the type of part to be washed, the degree of contamination and the chemical used. The manufacturer does not provide part-specific standard parameters or recipes. The parameters determined must be kept in the records of the operating company for each part type (**See Chapter 14.1**).

| Parameter | Setting location | Setting range | Determined by |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Process water temperature** | Thermostat (GEMO DT481) | Max. 70 °C | Operating company |
| **Washing time** | Timer (GEMO DZ482) | 0–100 min | Operating company |
| **Chemical type and concentration** | Tank filling | According to the chemical manufacturer's technical data sheet | Operating company |

When determining the parameters for a new part type, apply the following method:

1. Start with the temperature and concentration values recommended by the chemical manufacturer.
2. Carry out a trial wash with a short washing time.
3. After washing, check the parts against the cleanliness requirement.
4. If the cleanliness result is insufficient, increase the washing time step by step and repeat the trial.
5. Record the shortest time at which the required cleanliness level is reached, together with the temperature and concentration values.

A washing time longer than necessary increases energy consumption and reduces capacity; a chemical concentration higher than necessary may leave residue on the parts and shortens the service life of the process water.
