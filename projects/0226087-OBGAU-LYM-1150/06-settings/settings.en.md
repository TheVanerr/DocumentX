# 6. SETTINGS

This chapter defines the factory settings of the machine and the settings that may be made by the operating company. The machine has no mechanical, pneumatic or vacuum settings; the settings made by the user are limited to the process water temperature and the washing time. The settings of the safety functions and protective devices have been made at the factory and are not changed by the user.

| Section | Subject | Set by |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **6.1** | Mechanical settings | – (no setting) |
| **6.2** | Safety-related settings | Factory setting |
| **6.3** | Electrical settings | Authorised electrical personnel / operator |
| **6.4** | Hydraulic settings | – (no setting) |
| **6.5** | Pneumatic settings | Not applicable |
| **6.6** | Vacuum settings | Not applicable |
| **6.7** | Process parameters | Operating company |

The main factors that determine the washing result are the process water temperature, the washing time, the chemical concentration and the way the parts are arranged in the basket. These parameters influence one another: for example, a lower temperature may require a longer washing time or a higher chemical concentration. When changing one parameter, take the effect of the others into account and record every change.

**CAUTION — Unauthorised change of settings:** Changing the thermostat temperature limit, the thermal overload relay settings or the safety circuit causes the machine components to operate outside their design limits, creates a risk of injury and voids the warranty. Do not change any setting that is not defined in this chapter.
