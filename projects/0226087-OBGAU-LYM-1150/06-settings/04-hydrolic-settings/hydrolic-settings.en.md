# 6.4 Hydraulic settings

The machine has no hydraulic power unit. There is no pressure or flow rate setting to be made by the user in the washing water circuit (pump, pipes, spray arms); the pump flow rate is fixed according to the pump type (**See Chapter 3.3.3**).

The performance of the washing water circuit is maintained not by adjustment but by keeping the filters and nozzles clean. A clogged pre-filter or suction filter reduces the pump flow rate, which lowers the washing quality and overloads the pump (**See Chapter 10**).

As the machine has no permanent water inlet, there is no water inlet pressure to be set; the tank is filled manually (**See Chapter 5.3.2**).
