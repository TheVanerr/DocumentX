# 3.3 Technical specifications

This section gives the physical, process, electrical, utility and ambient values for the standard configuration of the LYM 1150 NS (serial no 0226087) machine. These tables are the single reference source of the manual for technical values; other chapters refer to these values.

For machines modified at the customer's request, the applicable values are specified in the as-built documents supplied with the machine. In the event of a discrepancy between the values in the tables and the type plate on the machine, **the values on the type plate** apply.

The type plate is located at the lower rear corner of the right-hand side surface of the machine, next to the CE marking. The plate shows the brand (OBG Automotive), model type (LYM 1150 NS), supply voltage and frequency (380 V / 50 Hz), total power (11 kW), maximum operating temperature, year of manufacture (2026) and serial number (0226087); the plate bears the CE and EAC markings. State the model and serial number in all correspondence with the manufacturer and in all spare part orders.

The maximum operating temperature stated on the type plate (85 °C) is the design limit of the machine. In operation, the process water temperature is set on the thermostat to a maximum of **70 °C**; this limit must not be exceeded (**See Chapter 3.4.8**).

## 3.3.6 Ambient conditions and emissions

| Parameter | Unit | Value |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Operating environment** | – | Indoor |
| **Operating ambient temperature** | °C | +10 / +30 |
| **Storage ambient temperature** | °C | +10 / +30 |
| **Relative humidity** | % | 30–50 |
| **Electrical equipment protection rating** | – | IP54 |
| **A-weighted sound pressure level** | dB(A) | 50 |

When the ambient temperature falls below +10 °C, there is a risk of the water in the tank, pump and pipes freezing; the machine must not be operated under these conditions, and the tank must be emptied during prolonged shutdowns (**See Chapter 4.2**).

During washing, and especially when the lid is opened, water vapour escapes from the washing chamber. The steam in the chamber is released through the two 3" PVC steam vent outlets in the upper part of the left and right side surfaces of the chamber; these outlets must not be closed or obstructed. On machines fitted with the steam extraction fan option, the steam is forced by the fan into an extraction duct connected to the facility ventilation or to the outside. The area in which the machine is installed must be ventilated so as to prevent the accumulation of steam.
