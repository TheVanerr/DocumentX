# 3.2 Intended use

This section defines the design purpose of the machine, the conditions under which and the persons by whom it may be used, and the reasonably foreseeable forms of misuse. The machine is considered safe only within the purpose and limits specified here. Any use outside these limits is regarded as use contrary to the intended purpose.

## 3.2.1 Purpose of use

The machine is designed and manufactured to remove oil, grease, swarf, carbon residue and similar process contaminants from the surfaces of industrial parts using heated process water consisting of a mixture of water and water-based alkaline washing chemical.

The machine is designed for indoor use in industrial facilities where manufacturing, casting, pressing, machining and maintenance and repair activities are carried out. The purpose of the machine is to bring parts to a level of cleanliness suitable for subsequent operations such as coating, painting, welding or assembly.

## 3.2.2 Processable parts and process liquid

The following parts can be washed in the machine:

- Metal parts
- Plastic parts

For parts to be considered washable, they must withstand water temperatures of up to 70 °C, the alkaline washing chemical and the spray pressure. Parts whose resistance to these conditions is not known must be assessed against the technical data of the part manufacturer before the first wash.

The total weight of the parts loaded into the basket must not exceed the maximum loading capacity of the model (**See Chapter 3.3.2**). Overloading causes the thermal protection of the basket gear motor to trip and causes permanent damage to the drive components.

Only the following process liquid may be used in the machine tank:

- Mains water
- Water-based alkaline washing chemical solution prepared with mains water and compatible with stainless steel, seal and pump materials

The chemical dosage and conditions of use are determined by the operating company in accordance with the safety data sheet (SDS) and technical data sheet of the chemical manufacturer.

## 3.2.3 Conditions of use and personnel

The machine may only be operated when all of the following conditions are met:

- The machine must be positioned in accordance with the space requirements defined in **Chapter 3.5** and installed in accordance with **Chapter 5**.
- The ambient temperature and relative humidity must be within the limits given in **Chapter 3.3.6**.
- All protective devices and safety functions (emergency stop, lid-closed switch, interlock lock if fitted) must be fitted and in working order.
- The machine must be used by operators who have read this manual and have been trained by the operating company. Maintenance and electrical work must be carried out only by authorised maintenance and electrical personnel (**See Chapter 1**).

The machine may be used by a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 5 operators at the same time. Where more than one operator is involved, control authority must rest with a single person, and lid opening, loading and cycle starting must be coordinated by this person.

## 3.2.4 Reasonably foreseeable misuse and prohibited practices

The following uses are reasonably foreseeable misuse and are **strictly prohibited**. These uses can result in serious injury, fire, explosion, environmental pollution or permanent damage to the machine.

| Prohibited use | Reason |
| :--- | :--- |
| Washing living organisms such as humans, animals or plants, or entering the washing chamber | Hot water, chemical exposure and the rotating basket can cause fatal injury. |
| Washing packaged or unpackaged food products or materials in direct contact with food | The machine is not designed to food hygiene requirements; the process water contains chemical and oil residues. |
| Cleaning, disinfecting or sterilising medical or surgical devices | The machine is not a steriliser and does not meet medical hygiene requirements. |
| Putting flammable or combustible liquids such as solvent, thinner, acetone, petrol, diesel or alcohol into the tank, or washing parts wet with such liquids | The heater and electrical equipment are ignition sources; the machine is not suitable for use in explosive atmospheres (ATEX). |
| Using acid-based chemicals or chemicals containing high concentrations of salt | Causes corrosion and loss of sealing on stainless steel surfaces, seals and the pump. |
| Washing wood, textiles, sponge, paper or similar fibrous and friable materials | Disintegrating material clogs the filters, the pump and the nozzles. |
| Raising the process water temperature above the 70 °C limit or changing the thermostat limit | The risk of scalding and steam burns increases; the pump and seals operate outside their design limits. |
| Forcing the heater or the pump to run when the tank is empty or the water level is low | The heater overheats and is damaged; the pump runs dry and the mechanical seal is damaged. |
| Exceeding the maximum loading capacity of the basket | The basket gear motor and drive components are subjected to overload. |
| Bridging or disabling the lid-closed switch, the interlock lock or the emergency stop circuit | The cycle can start with the lid open; injury results from sprayed hot water and the rotating basket. |
| Using the machine outdoors or outside the ambient conditions given in **Chapter 3.3.6** | The protection rating of the electrical equipment and the operating limits of the components are exceeded. |

## 3.2.5 Limitation of liability

The operating company is responsible for using the machine within the purpose and limits defined in this manual. In the event of use contrary to the intended purpose, any of the prohibited practices being carried out, or modification of the machine without the written approval of the manufacturer, the machine is excluded from the warranty.

The manufacturer cannot be held liable for personal injury, property damage, fire, explosion, environmental pollution or damage to the washed parts resulting from use contrary to the intended purpose. The scope and conditions of the warranty are defined in the warranty certificate supplied with the machine (**See Chapter 13.1**).
