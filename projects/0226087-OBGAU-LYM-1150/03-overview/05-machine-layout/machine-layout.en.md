# 3.5 Machine layout and space requirements

This section defines the minimum dimensions that the installation area must provide, the floor characteristics and the working and maintenance zones around the machine. The requirements given here have been determined to ensure safe operation of the machine, to provide maintenance access and to allow the operator to leave the working area unobstructed in an emergency. Before installation, the operating company must verify that the area complies with these requirements (**See Chapter 5**).

## 3.5.1 Working and access zones

The machine is operated from the front; no separate sides are defined for loading, unloading and control. Maintenance access is from the top lid, from the rear of the machine and from the side surfaces (**See Figure 3.4** and **3.5**).

| Zone | Location | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operator zone** | Front | Area where the lid is opened and parts are loaded and unloaded |
| **Loading / unloading** | Front | Parts are loaded into and unloaded from the basket through the top lid from the front |
| **Control** | Right | The electrical panel is on the right-hand side of the washing chamber (**See Chapter 3.4**) |
| **Maintenance access — top** | Top lid | The filters, basket, spray arms and tank interior are accessed from the top with the lid open |
| **Maintenance access — rear** | Rear | The frame compartment housing the washing pump and connecting pipes is open at the rear |
| **Maintenance access — sides** | Right and left | On the right, the electrical panel door, drain valve, basket gear motor housing and supply cable; on the left, the interlock lock box; on both sides, the steam vent outlets and gas springs |

The passage between the operator zone and the electrical panel must be kept clear so that the control elements and the emergency stop button can be reached without obstruction at all times. No materials, parts bins or handling equipment may be left in this zone.

## 3.5.2 Minimum clearances

The following minimum clearances must be left around the machine. The values are measured from the overall dimensions of the machine and from the outermost point, including the electrical panel. The overall dimensions of the machine are given in **Chapter 3.3.1**.

| Direction | Minimum clearance (mm) |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Front** | 1000 |
| **Rear** | 1000 |
| **Left and right sides** | 1000 |
| **Between machine top surface and ceiling** | 1000 |

The clearance at the front is required so that the operator can move safely during loading and unloading. The rear clearance is left for maintenance access to the pump compartment; the side clearances are left so that the electrical panel door can be opened, the drain valve and the interlock lock box can be reached, the steam vent outlets remain unobstructed and cleaning work can be carried out. The top clearance is required so that the lid can be fully opened without obstruction and the steam can disperse.

If a forklift or lifting equipment is to be used for loading and unloading parts, the clearance at the front must be increased to allow for the manoeuvring area of this equipment.

## 3.5.3 Floor requirements

The machine is placed on 4 fixed-height feet that do not require anchoring. As the feet have no height adjustment, the flatness of the floor must be ensured before installation.

| Parameter | Value |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Foot type** | 4 fixed-height feet |
| **Floor flatness tolerance** | ≤ 10 mm/m |
| **Alignment tolerance** | ≤ 10 mm |
| **Floor load capacity** | ≥ 100 kg/m² |

The floor must be capable of bearing the operating weight of the machine (tank full, basket loaded), be resistant to water and washing chemical, and have a non-slip surface. As water may drip onto the floor when the lid is opened and parts are unloaded, a non-slip floor covering or grating is recommended in the operator zone.

A drainage point or collection arrangement must be available around the machine so that the waste water can be safely discharged when the tank is emptied (**See Chapter 3.3.5**).

## 3.5.4 Lifting points and centre of gravity

The centre of gravity of the machine is at the centre of the machine body. The machine may be lifted only with a forklift, with the forks passed through the frame under the machine.

**WARNING — Tipping and falling:** The machine has no crane lifting points. Do not lift the machine with a crane, rope or sling; the body and lid can be deformed, and the machine can tip over or fall. Lift the machine only with a forklift from the frame underneath, balanced with respect to the centre of gravity.

Transport and handling instructions are given in **Chapter 4**.
