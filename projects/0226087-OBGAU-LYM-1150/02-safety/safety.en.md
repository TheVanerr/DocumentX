# 2. SAFETY

**LYM 1150** (order no. **0226087**), a rotating-basket industrial parts washing machine, involves risks arising from process water heated to up to 70 °C, the electric heating element inside the tank, the washing basket driven by the basket gear motor, the washing pump that sprays the process water under pressure, the 380 V three-phase electrical supply and exposure to alkaline washing chemical. This chapter defines the safety rules that must be followed during the installation, commissioning, operation, maintenance and servicing of the machine. The instructions have been prepared in accordance with the principles of EN ISO 12100 and EN ISO 20607.

Violation of the safety requirements creates a risk of injury, equipment damage, environmental impact and termination of warranty cover. The organisation operating the machine (the employer) is responsible for assigning and training personnel and for keeping the manual accessible (**See Chapter 1.1.3**).

As this machine has no PLC or HMI, the safety functions are entirely **hardware-based**: the emergency stop button on the electrical panel, the lid-closed switch on the washing chamber lid, the level switch in the tank and the thermal overload relays of the motors operate through a relay-based safety circuit. When the safety circuit opens, the return to the safe state is acknowledged only with the blue **RESET** button on the panel (**See Chapter 3.4.6**). This architecture leaves no possibility of a software bypass; however, physical interventions such as bridging a switch or disconnecting wiring completely disable the safety function and are **strictly prohibited**.
