# 9.1 Maintenance instructions

## 9.1.1 Maintenance safety

Ensure the following conditions before starting maintenance work:

1. Stop the machine with the STOP button and wait until the basket has come to a complete standstill.
2. Set the heater switch, the oil skimmer switch (if fitted) and the main switch to position **0**.
3. Apply LOTO (**See Chapter 2.4**).
4. If work is to be carried out inside the tank or the washing chamber, wait until the process water has cooled to a safe temperature.
5. If work is to be carried out with the lid open, check that the gas springs hold the lid; if work is to be carried out under the lid, secure the lid with a support (**See Chapter 9.1.7**).
6. Use the personal protective equipment required for the work (**See Chapter 2.6**).

After maintenance, refit all guards and covers that were removed. If work has been carried out on the safety circuit (emergency stop, lid switch, interlock, level switch), repeat the safety function tests before returning the machine to service (**See Chapter 5.4**).

## 9.1.7 Inspection and replacement of the lid gas springs

The top lid is held in the open position by two gas springs (2 × 350 N; **See Chapter 3.3.2**). Gas springs lose gas pressure over time; a gas spring with reduced force cannot hold the lid in the open position, and the lid can close by itself during loading or cleaning and cause injury to the hands, arms and head.

**WARNING — Falling lid:** With a gas spring that has lost its force, the lid closes unexpectedly; fingers and hands can be crushed and head injuries can occur. Do not skip the monthly inspection; do not use the machine without replacing a gas spring that does not hold the lid in the open position. Secure the lid in the open position with a support during inspection and replacement.

**Monthly inspection**

1. Stop the machine and apply LOTO (**See Chapter 2.4**).
2. Open the lid fully and release it; observe that the lid remains in the open position for at least 1 minute without sagging.
3. Close the lid slowly; check that the movement is even and smooth on both sides and that the gas springs do not bind.
4. Check the gas spring body for oil leakage and the piston rod for scratches, corrosion or bending.
5. Check that the gas spring connection pins, ball joint heads and mounting brackets are tight and undamaged.
6. Enter the inspection result in the maintenance record (**See Chapter 9.1.5, 14.1.5**).

**Replacement**

7. If the lid does not stay in the open position by itself, drops uncontrolled during closing, or a gas spring shows leakage or piston damage, replace both gas springs together (order code 10 11779; **See Chapter 13.3**).
8. The replacement is carried out by authorised maintenance personnel with the lid secured in the open position by a support; remove the gas spring only at its connection pins, do not open or drill its body.
9. After replacement, check that the lid remains in the open position and check the alignment of the lid-closed switch and the interlock lock (option) (**See Chapter 5.4.2**).

Gas springs contain pressurised gas; do not open, heat or drill a removed gas spring, and dispose of it in accordance with **Chapter 12.3**.

## 9.1.8 Annual electrical safety tests

The electrical equipment of the machine is tested once a year, and additionally after every repair or modification carried out in the electrical panel, by authorised electrical personnel in accordance with the Low Voltage Directive (LVD, 2014/35/EU) and EN 60204-1. The tests verify that the protective earthing, the insulation and the protective devices have not deteriorated over time; they reduce the risk of electric shock, leakage current and fire.

| No | Test | Method | Acceptance criterion |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Protective conductor (PE) continuity | Measurement of the resistance between the PE terminal of the supply plug and the machine frame, panel enclosure, motor housings and heating element flange | ≤ 0.1 Ω at each point |
| 2 | Insulation resistance | 500 V DC insulation measurement between the power circuit conductors and PE with the main switch off (electronic devices such as the thermostat, timer and power supply are disconnected from the measurement) | ≥ 1 MΩ |
| 3 | Residual current device | Tripping with the test button; checking the tripping current and tripping time with a test instrument | Tripping within ≤ 300 ms at 30 mA |
| 4 | Phase sequence relay and thermal overload relays | Checking the relay output and the thermal overload relay setting values (**See Chapter 6.3.3**) | Setting values unchanged; relay gives output |
| 5 | Emergency stop and safety circuit | Test procedure in **Chapter 5.4.2** | All tests passed |
| 6 | Visual inspection | Cable sheaths, cable glands, terminal tightness, condensation and contamination inside the panel, legibility of the labels | No damage; connections tight; labels legible |

Keep the test results together with the date, the measured values and the signature of the person who carried out the test (**See Chapter 14.1.5**). If a result does not meet the acceptance criterion, take the machine out of service and repeat the test after rectifying the fault (**See Chapter 11.3**).
