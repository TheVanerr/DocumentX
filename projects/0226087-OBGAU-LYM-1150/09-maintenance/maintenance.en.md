# 9. MAINTENANCE

This chapter defines the periodic maintenance operations, lubrication requirements, maintenance records and spare parts management required to keep the machine operating safely and efficiently. A **preventive maintenance** approach is applied to the machine: maintenance operations are carried out at the specified intervals, without waiting for a fault to occur.

Regular maintenance prevents pump faults caused by clogged filters, loss of washing quality due to worn nozzles and unnoticed loss of function of the safety devices. Failure to observe the maintenance intervals can lead to machine failures, reduced washing quality and loss of warranty.

Cleaning operations (filter cleaning, tank cleaning, process water replacement) are defined separately in **Chapter 10**. The periodic maintenance table in this chapter also includes the intervals of the cleaning operations.

**WARNING — Unexpected start-up:** Starting maintenance work without energy isolation can cause serious injury through rotation of the basket, operation of the pump or electric shock. Apply LOTO before all maintenance work (**See Chapter 2.4**).
