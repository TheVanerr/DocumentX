# 1.2 Symbols and labelling conventions

The labels in this user manual and on the machine body have been placed in accordance with international standards in order to keep operator safety at the highest level, to indicate possible risks and to show the correct operating conditions of the machine.

Before commissioning the machine, carrying out maintenance or handling heavy/hot parts such as the galvanised baskets, make sure you study the meanings of the following symbols and the actions they make mandatory.

| Symbol (label) | Category | Description | Required action / instruction |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| ![General hazard](../../assets/1.2.2/warning1.svg) | **Warning** | General hazard / Caution | Indicates areas of the machine or equipment that present a potential hazard. Move carefully in the working area and follow the related additional safety instructions. |
| ![Hot surface](../../assets/1.2.2/warning2.svg) | **Warning** | Hot surface contact hazard | There are surfaces on or around the machine that reach high temperatures. To avoid the risk of burns, do not touch these points with bare hands until the system has cooled down completely. |
| ![Corrosive substance](../../assets/1.2.2/warning3.svg) | **Warning** | Corrosive substance | Indicates areas where corrosive chemicals are present or used. Avoid direct contact with the chemicals and take precautions against the risk of spillage/splashing. |
| ![Crushing hazard](../../assets/1.2.2/warning4.svg) | **Warning** | Crushing / entrapment hazard | Indicates areas with moving mechanisms where there is a risk of limbs being trapped or crushed. Do not open the protective guards while the machine is running and keep away from moving parts. |
| ![Slippery floor](../../assets/1.2.2/warning5.svg) | **Warning** | Slippery floor hazard | There is a risk of a slippery floor in the working area caused by spilled liquid or leaks. Walk carefully to avoid the risk of falling. |
| ![Use of flammable substances prohibited](../../assets/1.2.2/warning6.jpg) | **Prohibition** | Flammable / combustible substances prohibited | The use of flammable or explosive liquids and open flames inside or around the machine is strictly prohibited. Use only substances approved by the manufacturer. |
| ![Read the manual](../../assets/1.2.2/kkd1.webp) | **Mandatory** | Refer to the user manual | Read the user manual in full and follow its instructions before commissioning the machine, putting it into operation or carrying out any maintenance work. |
| ![Protective overalls](../../assets/1.2.2/kkd2.svg) | **PPE** | Work clothing mandatory | Suitable work clothing or protective overalls that protect the body against potential risks must be worn during operation and maintenance. |
| ![Safety footwear](../../assets/1.2.2/kkd3.svg) | **PPE** | Steel-toe-capped footwear mandatory | Protective steel-toe-capped safety footwear must be worn in the working area to guard against the risks of falling materials, impact or crushing. |
| ![Protective gloves](../../assets/1.2.2/kkd4.svg) | **PPE** | Protective gloves mandatory | Protective gloves appropriate to the nature of the work must be worn when handling sharp-edged, hot or chemically contaminated surfaces/parts. |
| ![Protective goggles](../../assets/1.2.2/kkd5.svg) | **PPE** | Protective goggles mandatory | Suitable protective safety goggles must be worn to protect the eyes against the risk of liquid splashes, flying particles or chemical vapours. |
| ![Respirator mask](../../assets/1.2.2/kkd6.svg) | **PPE** | Respirator mask mandatory | A filtered respirator mask suited to the working environment must be worn to guard against the risk of inhaling harmful gases, vapours or particles that may be released into the air. |

> **Note:** Replace worn, removed or illegible warning labels on the machine with new ones immediately. Operating a machine without its labels is a breach of occupational safety.
