## 1.3.2 Service and technical support request procedure

So that the technical service can respond to the problem encountered as quickly, accurately and efficiently as possible, the following basic machine and fault information must be prepared in full before making contact:

1. **Machine identification details:** The **model name** (LYM 1150 NS), **serial number** (0226087) and **year of manufacture** on the type plate at the rear lower corner of the right-hand side of the machine (**See Chapter 3.3**).
2. **Fault or condition description:** A detailed description of the problem encountered. (For example: "The pump does not start", "The water does not heat up", "The basket does not rotate", "Washing does not start with the lid closed".)
3. **Fault lamps:** Which of the red fault lamps on the control panel is lit (INADEQUATE WATER LEVEL, REDUCTOR FAILURE, PUMP FAILURE) and the state of the RESET lamp (**See Chapter 3.4.10**).
4. **Operating conditions:** The operating state at the time the fault occurred (during the heating phase, during the washing cycle or at the end of the cycle; how long the machine had been running; the timer and thermostat settings).
5. **Visual documentation:** In the case of possible mechanical damage, leaks or abnormal conditions, sending the service clear photographs or videos of the affected area will considerably speed up the diagnostic process.

## 1.3.3 Spare parts supply and mandatory use of original parts

The safe, high-performance operation of the machine throughout its design life depends on the use of original spare parts approved by the manufacturer only.

* **Ordering process:** Spare part orders must be placed with OBG AUTOMOTIVE LTD with reference to the parts list in **Chapter 13.3** of this manual. When ordering, the **order code**, **part name**, **quantity required** and **machine serial number** of the part requested must be stated in writing.
* **Use of non-original parts:** Fitting equivalent, aftermarket or non-standard parts (nozzles, seals, heating element, lid switch, level switch, pump, gear motor, etc.) procured without the manufacturer's written approval to the machine is strictly prohibited. The use of non-original parts not only reduces the performance of the machine; it creates direct occupational safety risks and **immediately excludes the machine from warranty cover**.

## 1.3.4 Checks to be made before making contact

Before requesting service, the operator or maintenance personnel are advised to carry out the following basic checks in order to avoid possible loss of time and unnecessary service costs:

* Is the plant supply line switched on, is the supply plug inserted in the socket and is the main switch (POWER) in position **1**; is the phase sequence relay giving an output (**See Chapter 5.5.2**)?
* Has the emergency stop (EMERGENCY STOP) button been released, and does the reset lamp light when the RESET button is pressed (**See Chapter 2.5**)?
* Is the lid fully closed, is the lid latch closed and is the interlock lock in the **LOCK** position (**See Chapter 5.4.1**)?
* Is the water level in the tank sufficient; is the INADEQUATE WATER LEVEL lamp off (**See Chapter 7.2**)?
* Are the pre-filter and the suction filter clean and in place (**See Chapter 10**)?
* If the thermal overload relay or the residual current device has tripped, has the cause been rectified (**See Chapter 11**)?

## 1.3.5 Limits of the manufacturer's liability and prohibition of unauthorised intervention

The legal and technical liability of the manufacturer is limited to the operation of the machine for its intended use only, in accordance with the technical specifications, safety equipment and operating instructions stated in this manual.

Without the knowledge and written approval of the manufacturer or of the OBG AUTOMOTIVE LTD authorised service, any structural change, addition of equipment, removal or bypassing (bridging) of safety equipment or unauthorised modification carried out on the machine mechanics, the electrical panel and control circuit, the water circuit or the safety equipment is strictly prohibited. Because such unauthorised interventions directly endanger the safety standards of the machine, they immediately void all legal, financial and criminal liability of the manufacturer (including the warranty). If the machine is taken outside its safe operating limits, the organisation operating the machine is solely responsible for any accident that may occur.
