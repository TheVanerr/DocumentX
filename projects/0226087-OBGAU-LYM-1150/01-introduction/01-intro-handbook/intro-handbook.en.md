# 1.1 About this manual

## 1.1.1 Purpose, scope and field of application of the manual

This user manual is an integral, fundamental and legally binding component of the industrial washing machine. It has been prepared with great care, with due regard to the applicable Machinery Safety Regulation and the relevant international standards (EN ISO 12100, EN ISO 20607), to ensure that the machine is operated safely, efficiently, in an environmentally responsible manner and in the way best suited to its design purpose.

The scope of the document has been structured to cover the entire life cycle of the machine. This life cycle includes the following stages:
* **Transport and positioning:** Shipment of the machine from the factory, on-site handling, use of the slinging/lifting points and anchoring to the floor.
* **Installation and commissioning:** Connection of the site infrastructure such as electricity, waste water drainage and ventilation, and performance of the first test runs.
* **Operation:** Daily production routines, creation of washing recipes, loading, running and unloading of the machine.
* **Maintenance and cleaning:** Daily, weekly, monthly and annual periodic maintenance procedures, lubrication points, filter cleaning and inspection of wear parts.
* **Troubleshooting:** The meaning of the fault lamps on the control panel, first response actions in the event of a fault and the troubleshooting steps.
* **Decommissioning and disposal:** Safe de-energising, dismantling and disposal of the machine in accordance with recycling procedures once it has reached the end of its economic life.

This document is not intended to provide basic engineering, general mechanical or basic electrical training. It is assumed that all personnel who will work on the machine already hold the professional and technical training appropriate to their job description and have adopted a basic culture of industrial safety.

## 1.1.3 Target audience, personnel qualification and allocation of responsibilities

Because industrial washing machines involve high voltage, hot water, pressurised systems, chemical solutions and moving mechanical parts, the competence of the personnel who intervene on the machine is a critical occupational safety factor. The employer (the organisation operating the machine) is solely responsible for assigning personnel in accordance with the following authorisation matrix:

1. **Operator:**

   * **Authorisation:** Daily operation of the machine, loading and unloading of parts, setting the washing time and temperature on the control panel, and starting/stopping the washing cycle.
   * **Requirement:** Must have been trained by the employer in the operation of the machine and in the emergency stop procedures. The operator is strictly forbidden to remove the machine guards (covers) using tools, to open the electrical panel or to interfere with the parameter settings.

2. **Maintenance personnel (mechanical / electrical):**
   * **Authorisation:** Performance of the periodic maintenance steps specified in the manual, replacement of wear parts (filters, seals, etc.), sensor adjustments and basic fault diagnosis.
   * **Requirement:** Must hold a diploma/certificate in the relevant engineering or technical trade. Must have full command of the hazardous energy control procedures (equipment lockout and tagout – LOTO) and must use the appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) during maintenance. Electrical personnel must be authorised in accordance with the applicable national electrical installation regulations.

3. **Manufacturer's authorised service specialist:**
   * **Authorisation:** Intervention in the control circuit of the electrical panel and the protective device settings, replacement of main motors/pumps and major structural revisions.
   * **Requirement:** Only personnel who have been specifically trained and certified by the manufacturer and who hold a current certificate of authorisation.

## 1.1.5 Intended use, limitation of liability and voiding of warranty

The manufacturer has designed and manufactured the machine in accordance with recognised good engineering practice and strict safety standards. The warranty of the machine and the legal liability of the manufacturer are conditional on the system being operated only within the limits of the "intended use" for which it was designed.

In the event of direct or indirect personal injury, loss of life, plant damage, product loss, environmental pollution or loss of commercial profit that may arise from the usage errors, unauthorised interventions and operating faults detailed below (but not limited to these), the manufacturer accepts no legal, criminal or financial liability whatsoever; in such cases the machine is **immediately excluded from warranty cover**:

* **Use beyond capacity and purpose:** Operating the machine under strain above the maximum load, pressure, temperature and cycle capacity limits stated on the type plate and in the manual. Washing anything other than the specific industrial parts for which the machine was designed (for example explosive, flammable or highly reactive materials).
* **Safety violations:** Removing, bypassing (bridging), disabling in software or rendering inoperative vital occupational safety components such as the emergency stop button, the lid-closed switch, the interlock lock, the level switch, the safety circuit relays or the thermostat temperature limit.
* **Unauthorised modifications:** Making any change to the machine structure, the piping system, the electrical panel or the control circuit without the manufacturer's written and stamped approval.
* **Chemical and material incompatibility:** Using strongly acidic, highly caustic (alkaline) or solvent-based chemicals during washing that have not been tested and approved by the manufacturer and that may cause corrosion of the machine equipment or the baskets. (In particular, the baskets and carrier products used inside the machine have been galvanised, and the surface resistance of these parts differs from that of painted parts; the use of agents that dissolve the galvanised layer voids the entire mechanical warranty.)
* **Part replacements:** Mechanical fatigue, pump faults and losses of washing performance resulting from the arbitrary replacement of the standard nozzles, which spray the water in a thin flat jet, or of the optional angled fan nozzles with nozzles of a different flow rate (litres/minute) and spray angle characteristic without the approval of the manufacturer's engineering department. Use of any non-original spare parts and consumables.
* **Infrastructure and supply faults:** Inadequate or incorrect infrastructure connections originating from the plant; component failures caused by a non-standard earthing line, mains voltage fluctuations exceeding the tolerable limits (phase dip/loss) or unsuitable process water (quality, temperature, level).
* **Neglected maintenance:** Failure to comply with the daily, weekly and monthly periodic maintenance schedule specified in the manual; lubrication and cleaning procedures carried out by unqualified personnel and with the wrong equipment.
