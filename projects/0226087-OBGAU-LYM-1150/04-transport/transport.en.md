# 4. TRANSPORT, HANDLING AND STORAGE

This chapter defines the transport, lifting, on-site handling and storage rules to be applied from the time the machine is received from the manufacturer's plant until it is placed at the installation site. The machine is shipped as a single, fully assembled unit; no additional assembly work on the machine is required after transport.

The machine may be lifted only with a **forklift** and only from the **base frame underneath the machine**. The machine has no crane lifting points; lifting with a crane, slings, ropes or chains is prohibited. The centre of gravity of the machine is at the centre of the body (**See Chapter 3.5.4**).

Transport, lifting and handling operations must be carried out by personnel who hold a valid forklift operator licence and have been assigned to the task in accordance with the operating company's occupational health and safety rules. The lifting capacity of the forklift used must be greater than the empty weight of the machine (**See Chapter 3.3.1**).

**WARNING — Tipping or falling load:** A machine lifted from the wrong point, loaded off-balance or with water in its tank can tip over or slide off the forks; crushing can result in serious injury or death. Lift the machine only with the tank empty, from the base frame and balanced about its centre of gravity. Do not allow anyone under the raised load or within the travel area of the forklift.
