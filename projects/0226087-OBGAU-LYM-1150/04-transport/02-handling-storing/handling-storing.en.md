# 4.2 Storage

If the machine is not to be installed immediately after receipt or will not be used for an extended period, the following storage conditions must be applied. These conditions have been specified to prevent damage to the stainless steel surfaces, the seals, the pump and the electrical components from moisture, freezing and contamination.

## 4.2.1 Storage conditions

| Parameter | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Storage environment** | Enclosed, dry, dust-protected area |
| **Ambient temperature** | +10 °C / +30 °C |
| **Relative humidity** | 30–50 % |
| **Storage position** | Upright, on all four feet |
| **Maximum storage period** | 12 months |

Do not store the machine in open areas where it would be exposed to rain, snow or direct sunlight. In areas where the ambient temperature falls below +10 °C, water remaining in the pump, the tank and the pipework can freeze and crack these components. Leave the machine upright on the floor with all four feet bearing evenly; do not stack loads on top of the machine.

Machines stored for longer than 12 months must be inspected by the manufacturer or the authorised service before commissioning.

## 4.2.2 Preparation for storage

If the machine is to be stored after it has been used, carry out the following steps:

1. Stop the machine and apply LOTO (**See Chapter 2.4**).
2. Drain the process water in the washing tank completely through the drain valve.
3. Clean the tank, the filters and the washing chamber in accordance with **Chapter 10**.
4. Allow the inner surfaces of the tank, the chamber and the filters to dry completely. Odour, deposits and surface staining form on surfaces left wet.
5. Wipe the outer surface of the machine with a dry cloth.
6. Close and lock the electrical panel door.
7. Cover the machine with a breathable, dust-repellent cover. Do not wrap the machine tightly in airtight plastic sheeting; condensation forming under the cover causes corrosion on the surfaces.

## 4.2.3 Removal from storage

Before commissioning a machine that has been in storage, carry out the following checks:

1. Remove the cover; visually inspect the body, the lid and the panel for any damage that has occurred during storage.
2. Check the supply cable, the plug and the panel cable entries for crushing, cuts or rodent damage.
3. Check that there is no dust, foreign matter or deposits inside the tank, the filter housings and the washing chamber.
4. Check that the lid gas springs hold the lid in the open position.
5. Position, connect and commission the machine in accordance with **Chapter 5**.
