# 5. INSTALLATION AND COMMISSIONING

This chapter defines the assembly, positioning, utility connections, safety function tests and commissioning checks to be carried out from the placement of the machine at the installation site until its first start-up. As the machine is shipped as a single, fully assembled unit, the installation work consists of positioning, electrical and drain connections, filling of the tank and function tests.

| Section | Subject | Carried out by |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5.1** | Machine assembly and unpacking | Installation personnel |
| **5.2** | Positioning and levelling | Installation personnel |
| **5.3** | Electrical, water and drain connections | Authorised electrical personnel, installation personnel |
| **5.4** | Testing the safety functions | Authorised electrical personnel |
| **5.5** | Installation check and trial run | Installation personnel |
| **5.6** | Communication connections | – |

Installation is completed by a one-person team in approximately 3 hours. Carry out the sections in the order given above. Do not proceed to the next step until the previous step has been completed and checked; do not operate the machine for production until the checklist in **Chapter 5.5** has been completed in full.

Installation work must be carried out by installation personnel who have read this manual and are familiar with the construction of the machine. Electrical connections and all checks on the electrical circuit must be carried out by electrical personnel authorised in accordance with the electrical regulations in force.

**WARNING — Electric shock:** Contact with the live supply line or with the conductors inside the panel can cause serious injury or death by electric shock. Before making the connections, switch off, lock out and tag out the supply circuit on the plant side; verify with a measuring instrument that the power has been isolated (**See Chapter 2.4**).

If you encounter a situation not described in this manual during installation, stop the work at a safe point and contact the manufacturer (**See Chapter 1.3**).
