# 5.2 Positioning and levelling

Correct positioning of the machine is necessary for balanced rotation of the basket, even distribution of the water in the tank, complete closing of the lid and correct signalling by the lid-closed switch. As the machine feet are of fixed height, levelling is achieved by preparing the floor, not by adjusting the feet.

## 5.2.1 Checking the installation site

Before placing the machine, check that the installation site meets the following conditions:

- The area provides the minimum clearances given in **Chapter 3.5.2**.
- The floor meets the flatness and load-bearing capacity requirements given in **Chapter 3.5.3**.
- The ambient conditions are within the limits given in **Chapter 3.3.6**.
- The electrical supply point (wall socket) and the waste water drain point are within reach of the machine; as the tank is filled by hand, there is a point nearby from which mains water can be drawn.

## 5.2.2 Placement and levelling

1. Lower the machine onto the installation site with the forklift (**See Chapter 4.1.3**).
2. Position the machine so that the control panel is within reach of the operator and the front face faces the operator area (**See Chapter 3.5.1**).
3. Check that all four feet are bearing fully on the floor. If one of the feet is not in contact with the floor, lift the machine and correct the unevenness of the floor.
4. Using a spirit level, take measurements on the top edge of the machine in the front-to-back and left-to-right directions. The levelling deviation must not exceed **10 mm**.
5. If the deviation exceeds 10 mm, lift the machine and correct the floor. Do not place makeshift shims under the feet; shims can work loose under vibration and unbalance the machine.
6. Open and close the lid; check that the lid seats correctly on the body and that the lid latch closes easily.

The machine is not anchored to the floor. Check during the first trial run that the machine does not shift from its position during operation (**See Chapter 5.5**).
