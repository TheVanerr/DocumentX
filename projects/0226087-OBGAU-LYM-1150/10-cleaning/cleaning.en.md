# 10. CLEANING

This chapter defines the cleaning of the machine's filters, washing tank, washing chamber and external surfaces, the replacement of the process water and the disposal of cleaning waste. The machine has no clean-in-place (CIP) or clean-out-of-place (COP) system; all cleaning operations are carried out by hand.

Regular cleaning of the filters directly determines the washing performance of the machine. Clogged filters reduce the amount of water reaching the pump, lower the spray pressure and cause the pump to overload and the thermal overload protection to trip. A dirty tank leaves residue on the parts and causes odour.

The cleaning intervals are included in the periodic maintenance table in **Chapter 9.1.3**. Cleaning operations may be carried out by trained operators.
