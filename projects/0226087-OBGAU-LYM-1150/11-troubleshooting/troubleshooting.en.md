# 11. TROUBLESHOOTING

This chapter defines the diagnosis and rectification of the faults that can occur on the machine. The machine has no display showing alarm codes; faults are detected by the three red fault lamps on the control panel (INADEQUATE WATER LEVEL, REDUCTOR FAILURE, PUMP FAILURE) and by the behaviour of the machine (**See Chapter 3.4.10**).

| :--- | :--- |
| **11.1** | General diagnostic sequence and criteria for calling the service |
| **11.2** | Fault table |
| **11.3** | Electrical faults |
| **11.4** | Water circuit and pump faults |
| **11.5** | Pneumatic faults |
| **11.6** | Vacuum faults |
| **11.7** | Sensor faults |

At the beginning of each diagnostic step it is stated who may carry it out; the operator carries out only the steps defined for the operator.

**WARNING — Electric shock and unexpected start-up:** Stop the machine and apply LOTO before any intervention inside the electrical panel or in the pump or gear motor area (**See Chapter 2.4**). Measurements inside the live panel may only be carried out by authorised electrical personnel, using suitable test instruments and personal protective equipment.
