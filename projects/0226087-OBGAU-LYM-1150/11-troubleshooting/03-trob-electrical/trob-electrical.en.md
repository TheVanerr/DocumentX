# 11.3 Electrical faults

This section summarises the behaviour of the machine's electrical protective devices. The diagnosis and rectification of electrical faults is carried out only by authorised electrical personnel. The electrical diagram is included in the document package supplied with the machine (**See Chapter 13.2**).

| Subject | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Motor protection** | The washing pump and the basket gear motor are protected by thermal overload relays. The thermal overload relay interrupts the circuit in the event of overcurrent or overheating. Pump and gear motor thermal overload trips are indicated by the PUMP FAILURE and REDUCTOR FAILURE lamps on the panel (**See Chapter 11.2**, Faults 1–2). |
| **Thermal overload relay settings** | Thermal overload relay setting values of the washing pump and the basket gear motor: [EKSİK] |
| **Optional motors** | The drying fan, oil skimmer, steam extraction fan and drain pump are protected by separate thermal overload relays; there is no fault lamp (**See Chapter 11.2**, Fault 4). |
| **Periodic test** | Protective conductor continuity, insulation resistance, the residual current device and the safety circuit are tested once a year (**See Chapter 9.1.8**). |
| **Residual current** | The residual current device interrupts the entire supply when an insulation fault occurs in any circuit (**See Chapter 11.2**, Fault 5). |
| **Phase sequence** | If the phase sequence is wrong, the phase sequence relay does not allow the machine to operate (**See Chapter 5.5.2**). |
| **Phase loss** | Machine behaviour in the event of phase loss: [EKSİK]. Refer to the electrical diagram (**See Chapter 13.2**). |
| **Frequency inverter** | None; all motors are connected direct-on-line (DOL). |
| **Cable colour codes** | Refer to the electrical diagram (**See Chapter 13.2**). |

Do not restart the machine by resetting the thermal overload relay without rectifying the cause of the overcurrent. Do not increase the thermal overload relay setting values; a higher setting value leaves the motor unprotected.
