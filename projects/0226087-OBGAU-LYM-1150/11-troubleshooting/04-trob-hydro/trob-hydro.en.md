# 11.4 Water circuit and pump faults

The machine has no hydraulic power unit and no pressure switch. This section covers the faults that can occur in the washing water circuit (tank, pump, pipes, nozzles).

| Symptom | Possible cause | Remedy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pump does not run** | Pump thermal overload relay tripped | **Chapter 11.2**, Fault 2 |
| **Abnormal pump noise** | Blockage on the suction side | Clean the pre-filter and the suction filter (**See Chapter 10**). |
| **Abnormal pump noise** | Dry running due to low water level | **Chapter 11.2**, Fault 3 |
| **Abnormal pump noise** | Bearing damage or foreign object in the pump | Stop using the machine; contact the service. |
| **Leakage** | Wear of the pump mechanical seal, tank connections or valve seals | Locate the leak; have the seal or mechanical seal replaced. A leak can cause the pump to strain and the thermal overload relay to trip. |
| **Washing quality reduced, spray weak** | Clogged filter or nozzles | Clean the filters (**See Chapter 10**); check the nozzles for clogging. |

There is no low pump pressure alarm. If the pump does not run, apply **Chapter 11.2**, Fault 2.
