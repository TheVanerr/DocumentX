# 13.2 Technical drawings

The technical drawings of the machine are supplied as separate documents in the delivery drawing package. The table below shows the contents of the delivery drawing package.

| Drawing | File name / revision |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Electrical diagram** | [EKSİK] |
| **General assembly drawing** | [EKSİK] |
| **Layout drawing** | [EKSİK] |
| **Transport trolley assembly drawing** | [EKSİK] |

A lifting points drawing and a floor anchoring drawing do not apply to this machine: the machine is lifted only by the base frame with a forklift (**See Chapter 3.5.4**) and is not anchored to the floor (**See Chapter 5.2**).

Keep a copy of the electrical diagram in the document pocket on the door of the electrical panel. Every modification made to the electrical circuit must be entered on the diagram by authorised electrical personnel.
