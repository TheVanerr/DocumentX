# 8. CAPACITY AND PART-SPECIFIC SETTINGS

This chapter describes the factors that determine the washing capacity of the machine and how the part-specific process settings are managed. The machine is a general-purpose machine designed for washing parts of different geometries and sizes; for this reason the hourly capacity is not defined as a fixed value but is determined according to the part being washed.
