# 7.2 Machine start-up

This section describes energising the machine and heating the process water before the start of daily operation. It takes approximately **1 hour** for the process water to reach the target temperature; take this heating time into account in shift planning.

## 7.2.1 Pre-start checks

Before energising the machine, check the following:

1. Check that the power supply plug is plugged into the socket and that the cable is undamaged.
2. Check that the water level in the tank is sufficient. If the level is low, add water to the tank (**See Chapter 5.3.2**).
3. Check that the drain valve is closed.
4. Check that the pre-filter and the suction filter are in place and clean (**See Chapter 10**).
5. Check that the emergency stop button is in the released (not latched) position.

## 7.2.2 Energising and heating

1. Turn the main switch to position **1**.
2. Press the RESET button until the button lamp lights up. If the lamp does not light up, have the residual current device (RCD) and the circuit breakers checked (**See Chapter 11**).
3. Check that none of the fault lamps is lit. If a fault lamp is lit, do not operate the machine and rectify the fault in accordance with **Chapter 11**.
4. Check that the target temperature (SV) on the thermostat is at the value specified for the process (**See Chapter 6.3.1**).
5. Turn the heater switch to position **1**.
6. Check that the measured temperature (PV) on the thermostat display has started to rise.
7. Wait until the measured temperature reaches the target temperature.

Once the process water has reached the target temperature, the machine is ready for the washing cycle (**See Chapter 7.4**). The heater may be left switched on throughout the day; the thermostat keeps the water temperature at the target value.

**WARNING — Hot surface and hot water:** Once heating has started, the tank water, the inner surfaces of the washing chamber and the inner surface of the lid can heat up to 70 °C. If you need to open the lid during heating, keep your face away from the lid opening and wear heat-resistant protective gloves. Do not put your hand into the tank.
