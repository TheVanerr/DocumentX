# 7.5 Daily operating routine

The daily working hours, the shift pattern and the shift handover rules are determined by the operating company. The machine is suitable for continuous operation 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (**See Chapter 3.3.3**).

The following table summarises the recommended sequence of operations for the daily use of the machine. Detailed instructions are given in the relevant sections.

| Time | Operation | Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Start of shift** | Pre-start checks; energising; switching on the heater | **Chapter 7.2** |
| **Heating time (approximately 1 hour)** | Waiting for the process water to reach the target temperature | **Chapter 7.2.2** |
| **During production** | Loading, washing cycle, unloading | **Chapter 7.4** |
| **During production** | Monitoring the tank water level and the fault lamps | **Chapter 3.4.10** |
| **End of shift** | Shutting down the machine | **Chapter 7.3.2** |
| **End of shift** | Daily cleaning (pre-filter) | **Chapter 10** |
| **Weekend** | Weekly cleaning (suction filter, bag filter where fitted, tank surface) | **Chapter 10** |

At shift handover, the outgoing operator must inform the incoming operator of any abnormalities observed on the machine (noise, leakage, lit fault lamp), the tank water level and the date of the last cleaning. The shift handover form is prepared by the operating company.
