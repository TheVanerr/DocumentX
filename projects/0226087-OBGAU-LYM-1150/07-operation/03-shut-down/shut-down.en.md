# 7.3 Stopping and shutting down the machine

This section describes the normal stopping of the washing cycle, shutting down the machine at the end of the shift and preparing it for a prolonged shutdown. Stopping the machine in an emergency and restarting it after an emergency stop are defined in **Chapter 2.5**.

## 7.3.1 Stopping the washing cycle

The washing cycle stops automatically when the time set on the timer has elapsed. To end the cycle before the time has elapsed:

1. Press the red STOP button.
2. Wait until the basket has come to a complete stop.
3. Open the lid.

**WARNING — Rotating basket and hot steam:** Opening the lid while the basket is rotating can lead to a hand or arm being trapped between the basket and the chamber wall and to hot water splashing out. Open the lid only after the basket has come to a complete stop. Keep your face away from the chamber opening when opening the lid; the steam escaping from the chamber can cause burns.

Do not use the emergency stop button to stop the machine in normal operation (**See Chapter 3.4.2**).

## 7.3.2 Shutdown at the end of the shift

1. Complete the washing cycle in progress or stop it with the STOP button.
2. Unload the parts from the basket.
3. Turn the heater switch to position **0**.
4. Turn the main switch to position **0**.
5. Carry out the daily cleaning tasks (**See Chapter 10**).

## 7.3.3 Prolonged shutdown

If the machine is not going to be used over a weekend or for a longer period, carry out the following steps:

1. Stop the machine with the STOP button and wait until the basket has stopped.
2. Open the lid and unload the basket.
3. Turn the heater switch and the main switch to position **0**; apply LOTO (**See Chapter 2.4**).
4. Wait until the process water has cooled to a safe temperature.
5. Drain the water from the tank through the drain valve.
6. Clean and dry the tank and the washing chamber (**See Chapter 10**).

If the machine is to be put into storage, carry out the storage preparation described in **Chapter 4.2**.
