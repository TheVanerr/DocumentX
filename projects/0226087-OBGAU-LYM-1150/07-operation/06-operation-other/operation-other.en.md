# 7.6 Other operating information

## 7.6.1 Operator intervention points

In normal operation of the machine, the operator intervenes only at the following points:

| Intervention point | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Front of the control panel** | Starting, stopping, temperature and time setting, reset |
| **Top lid** | Loading and unloading parts |
| **Basket** | Arranging the parts |
| **Tank (with the lid open)** | Checking the water level and adding water |
| **Pre-filter** | Daily cleaning (**See Chapter 10**) |

The inside of the electrical panel, the pump and gear motor area and the pipe connections inside the frame are not within the operator's area of intervention. Work in these areas is carried out only by authorised maintenance or electrical personnel, with LOTO applied (**See Chapter 2.4**).

## 7.6.2 Monitoring the process water

With every washing cycle, the process water becomes loaded with the oil and dirt removed from the parts. Contaminated process water lowers the washing quality, accelerates clogging of the filters and leaves residue on the parts. Replace the process water when one of the following conditions is observed:

- Visible oil or dirt residue remaining on the washed parts,
- A thick layer of oil forming on the tank surface (on machines without an oil skimmer),
- A distinct odour developing in the process water,
- Expiry of the replacement interval recommended by the chemical manufacturer.

Replacing the process water and cleaning the tank are described in **Chapter 10**. The process water replacement interval is determined by the operating company depending on the quantity of parts washed and the degree of contamination.

## 7.6.3 Product changeover and waste management

No change of machine settings is required to switch between different part types; only the washing time and temperature specified for the new part type are set (**See Chapter 6.7**). Re-washing or segregating parts that do not reach sufficient cleanliness after washing is carried out in accordance with the quality procedures of the operating company.
