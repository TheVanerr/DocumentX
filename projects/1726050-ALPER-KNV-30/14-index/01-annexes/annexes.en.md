# 14.1 Annexes

Annexes refer to supporting documents that are outside the manual or in specific sections inside the manual. Procedure texts are not repeated; the reader is directed to the relevant section.

---

## 14.1.1 Annex list

| Annex | Topic | Location / delivery | Note |
|----|------|----------------|-----|
| **Annex A** | Alarm list (31 codes) | Section **11.1.2** — in the manual | PLC alarm texts verbatim |
| **Annex B** | Parameter list | Section **6.3** — HMI settings page | Not delivered as a separate document |
| **Annex C** | Recipe examples | Section **8.2** — user company | Process parameters are defined by the plant |
| **Annex D** | Warranty document | Separate document — delivery package | Claims and conditions with the manufacturer — see **1.3** |

---

## 14.1.2 Forms and tables in the manual

| Content | Section | Use |

| Scrap material sorting | **12.3.2** | Dismantling |
