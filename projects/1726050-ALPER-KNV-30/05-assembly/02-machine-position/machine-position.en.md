# 5.2 Machine positioning

Positioning determines the machine’s final location, orientation and level in the installation area. The operations are carried out under **Chapter 5.1 Step 3**; this section details them. Incorrect positioning adversely affects robot access, maintenance-cover opening and conveyor alignment.

Area requirements (clearances, ceiling height, orientation definitions) are given in **Chapter 3.5**; this section focuses on the installation procedure.

---

## 5.2.1 Installation area requirements

Before the machine is placed, the installation area must meet the following conditions:

| Parameter | Requirement | Reference |
|-----------|------------|------|
| Assembly area min. size | 5 m × 3 m | Chapter 3.5 |
| Floor flatness tolerance | 0.5 mm/m | Chapter 3.5 |
| Floor strength | Hard and level surface | Chapter 3.5.2 |
| Minimum clearance | Front, rear, sides: 1000 mm | Chapter 3.5.2 |
| Minimum ceiling height | 2500 mm | Chapter 3.5.2 |

The reference layout is the machine layout PDF in the delivery package (see **Chapter 3.5**).

## 5.2.2 Orientation definitions and placement

The machine must be positioned according to the following orientations (see **Chapter 3.5.1**):

| Definition | Direction |
|-------|-----|
| Operator side | Right |
| Infeed side (inlet) | Left |
| Outfeed side (outlet) | Right |
| Conveyor flow direction | Left → Right |

Parts are loaded from the left and taken from the right. The HMI panel and electrical cabinet are accessible on the operator side (right). In this project, infeed/outfeed is by **robot**; leave sufficient clearance on the infeed and outfeed sides when planning robot manoeuvring space.

Forklift placement is carried out according to the **Chapter 4.1.4** procedure; the centre of gravity is at the centre of the conveyor (see **Chapter 3.5.4**).

## 5.2.3 Levelling and alignment

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Levelling mechanism | Adjustable feet |
| Alignment tolerance | 0.5 mm |

### Positioning procedure

1. Bring the machine to its final position with a forklift and set it on the floor (see **Chapter 4.1.4**).
2. Using the **adjustable feet**, adjust the machine so that it is **level**.
3. Check both axes with a spirit level or equivalent measuring instrument.
4. Alignment tolerance must not exceed **0.5 mm**; if there is deviation, adjust foot heights and repeat the measurement.
5. Verify that all feet contact the floor evenly.

**Expected result:** The machine is level; the conveyor line is aligned with the target line.

**Abnormal condition:** If floor tolerance is exceeded, do not proceed to operation until the floor is corrected.

Mechanical installation test question: *Is the machine level?* (see **Chapter 5.5.1**)

## 5.2.4 Maintenance access

During positioning, maintenance access zones must not be obstructed (see **Chapter 3.5.3**):

| Zone | Requirement |
|-------|------------|
| Rear of the machine | All covers removable and accessible |
| Rear minimum clearance | 1000 mm |

If the machine is placed too close to a wall or equipment, filter maintenance and tank intervention cannot be performed safely.
