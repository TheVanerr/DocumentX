# 5. ASSEMBLY

Placement of the **KNV 30 3000 2B** (serial no. **1726050**) machine in the installation area, media and electrical connections, safety function tests and commissioning verification are defined in this chapter. Assembly is carried out after transport is complete (**See Chapter 4**) and before operation (**See Chapter 7**). Electrical connections must be made only by authorised electrical personnel.

Assembly operations take an estimated **1 day** and are performed by a **1-person** team. A **forklift** is used for placement; a **crane must not be used** (see **Chapter 3.5.4**). Dimensions, weight, electrical and media values are given in **Chapter 3.3**; the installation area and clearances are defined in **Chapter 3.5.2**.
