# 1. INTRODUCTION
 
**Model:** KNV-30 | **Machine:** KNV 30 3000 2B

This chapter defines the legal framework, conditions of validity, target audience and document-use rules of the user manual prepared for the **KNV 30 3000 2B** industrial parts washing machine. The manual clarifies which information personnel shall consult, in which order and under which responsibility during safe operation, maintenance and decommissioning of the machine.

The machine is a conveyor-type, two-bath (wash + rinse) system with infeed loading. Parts travel on the conveyor and complete washing, rinsing and drying processes. This definition limits the machine configuration for which the manual is valid; out-of-scope modifications or different option sets remain outside this text.

This user manual is an integral and legally binding part of the **KNV 30 3000 2B** machine; it has been prepared in accordance with the requirements of EN ISO 12100 and EN ISO 20607. The instructions in the manual are not advisory; you are obliged to follow them during operation, installation and maintenance. National occupational health and safety legislation remains applicable; in case of conflict, the stricter provision applies. Purpose, scope, validity, target audience, storage and allocation of responsibilities are detailed in **Chapter 1.1**.

The manual covers the entire life cycle of the machine from transport to disposal; procedures for each stage are given in the relevant chapters (**See Chapter 4–12**). Out-of-scope modification, use other than intended use or unauthorised intervention lie outside the limits of intended use; their consequences are the operating organisation’s responsibility (**See Chapter 1.1.5, 3.2**).

Safety rules, energy isolation (LOTO), emergency stop, PPE and residual risks are defined in **Chapter 2**; they are not repeated in other chapters. The employer is responsible for personnel selection, training and authorisation; the manufacturer’s responsibility is limited for consequences outside operation in accordance with intended use and the manual instructions (**See Chapter 1.1.3, 1.1.5, 2**). This manual does not provide basic engineering or electrical training; personnel who intervene on the machine are assumed to have the technical competence appropriate to their job description.
