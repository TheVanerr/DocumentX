# 6.6 Vacuum settings

The **KNV 30 3000 2B** machine has **no vacuum system**. Part holding, transfer or process steps are not performed with vacuum; part flow is provided by conveyor and robot integration.

There is **no** vacuum pressure, vacuum pump or vacuum-valve setting for the operator or maintenance personnel. A vacuum connection is not required among commissioning prerequisites (see **Chapter 7.2** — Air/water opening).

---

## 6.6.1 Vacuum settings status

| Parameter | Value / Description |
|-----------|------------------|
