# 11.4 Hydraulic faults

There is **no hydraulic system** on the **KNV 30 3000 2B** machine. The process is executed with compressed air and electric motors. This subsection does not apply.

| Parameter | Status |
|-----------|-------|
| Pressure-low alarm | Not applicable |
| Oil leak points | Not applicable |
| Abnormal pump noise (hydraulic) | Not applicable |

For pump faults see **Section 11.3.2** (electric motors).
