# 4.1 Transport and shipping

The machine is shipped in **container** packaging. Assembled transport weight is **1300 kg**; dismantled-part transport is not envisaged within this project — no module is separated during transport. The lifting appliance is a **forklift fork**; forklift fork entry **exists** on the machine (lower transport profiles). Transport securing and lashing are provided by the controlled lift/lower procedure with forklift; a separate spreader, sling or crane is not used.

External dimensions shall be taken into account in installation-area planning (see **Chapter 3.3.1**). The centre of gravity is at the middle of the conveyor line; fork position shall be adjusted accordingly (see **Chapter 3.5.4**).

---

## 4.1.1 Requirements before transport

The following conditions shall be met before starting transport. Stop transport if a condition is missing; do not continue until it is complete.

| Parameter | Value / Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Transport vehicle | Forklift (crane prohibited) |
| Transport weight — assembled | 1300 kg |
| Transport weight — dismantled max. part | 1300 kg (parts are not removed) |
| Packaging type | Container |
| Lifting appliance | Forklift fork |
| Forklift fork entry | Yes — lower profiles |
| Transport temperature | +10°C – +30°C |
| Environment | Moisture and corrosive substances shall not be present |
| Max. transport height (sea/land) | Machine external height **2122 mm** (see **Chapter 3.3.1**). A fixed transport-height limit is not defined; the route shall be planned according to open-top vehicle, container and entry-passage height |

The forklift operator must hold a valid certificate. Forklift safe working load (SWL) shall cover the **1300 kg** transport weight. Door height, ramp slope and turning radius on the transport path shall be suitable for the machine external dimensions (**3770 × 1730 × 2122 mm**) (see **Chapter 3.3.1**).

**CAUTION — Crane prohibition:** There is no crane lifting point or slinging equipment on the machine. Crane use creates chassis deformation, cabinet damage and tip-over risk.

---

## 4.1.2 Preparation before transport

1. Determine the transport route and destination in advance; check obstacles, slope and floor load capacity.
2. Verify that the plant **electrical, water and compressed-air** connections of the machine have been disconnected.
3. Check that the main switch is in the **OFF (0)** position.
4. Empty process fluid in the wash and rinse tanks before long-distance transport or storage; transporting a full tank changes the centre of gravity and creates a tip-over risk (see **Chapter 10.1.5**).
5. Check that no hanging cable, hose or loose part remains.
6. Check that the container packaging is suitable for transport, undamaged and that securing is adequate.
7. Assign the forklift operator and a banksman (if required).

---

## 4.1.3 Opening the container packaging

When the machine arrives at the installation area, packaging is opened in a controlled way so as to prevent transport damage:

1. Before opening the packaging, check for external damage (crushing, moisture, tip-over marks); if abnormal, photograph and report to plant management.
2. Remove packaging elements (screws, straps, corner protectors) without damaging the machine body.
3. Before lifting the machine with the forklift, verify that the lower profiles and feet are free of packaging elements.
4. Separate packaging waste in accordance with plant recycling rules.

---

## 4.1.4 Forklift transport procedure

**WARNING — Tip-over and crushing:** Incorrect fork position, excessive speed or unbalanced loading can lead to the machine tipping over during transport or to crushing of personnel. Seat the forks only in the lower transport profiles; align the centre of gravity to the middle of the conveyor (see **Chapter 3.5.4**).

1. Align the forklift forks with the machine **lower transport profiles**; do not apply load to outer cladding, electrical cabinet, pipe lines or fan ducts.
2. Adjust fork width and position according to the conveyor **centre of gravity**.
3. Lift the machine **10–15 cm** from the floor and perform a balance test; if there is leaning, front/rear imbalance or fork slip, lower slowly and correct the position.
4. Keep the load low throughout transport; do not make sudden turns, reverse manoeuvres or hard braking.
5. Use a banksman if the forklift field of view is restricted; do not keep personnel under the suspended load or on the load movement path.
6. Lower the machine slowly at the destination; check that all **adjustable feet** sit equally on the floor.
7. After placing in the installation area, proceed to **Chapter 5** — Assembly procedure.

**Expected result:** The machine shall be positioned at the destination in a balanced and undamaged state; the feet shall make full contact with the floor.

**Abnormal situation:** If tip-over, cracking or cabinet damage is detected, do not operate the machine; contact manufacturer service (see **Chapter 1.3**).

---

## 4.1.5 Check after transport

1. Check chassis, cabinet, covers and pipe lines for visible mechanical damage.
2. Verify that the feet and lower profiles show no deformation.
3. Check that the electrical-cabinet door is closed and dry.
4. If there is no damage, proceed to installation (see **Chapter 5.1**); if there is damage, record it and make a service notification.

Installation-area floor and surrounding-clearance requirements are given in **Chapter 3.5.2**.
