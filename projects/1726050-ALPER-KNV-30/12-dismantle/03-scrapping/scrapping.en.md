# 12.3 Scrap evaluation and recycling

After dismantling, parts must be classified by material group and evaluated in accordance with the **environmental disposal** and **recycling** legislation of the country of use. There is no battery. Oily process water, conveyor grease, gearbox-seal leakage and cleaning-detergent waste are segregated as waste.

---

## 12.3.1 Scrap evaluation

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Scrap evaluation | Sorting by material group must be performed |

### Scrap evaluation procedure

1. Separate the dismantled parts physically.
2. Determine the material group according to the **12.3.2** table below.
3. Ship to locally licensed disposal/recycling facilities.
4. Keep waste-transfer records under the plant environmental permits.
5. If original packaging (container) is present, take it into a separate recycling stream.

**Expected result:** Material groups separated; oily water and grease not mixed; disposal documented.

---

## 12.3.2 Part recycling materials

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Part recycling materials | Current recycling legislation of the country of use |

| Material group | Example parts on the machine | Disposal / recycling |
|---------------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Stainless steel / metal** | Tank body, chassis, conveyor, filter frames | To a metal recycling facility |
| **Plastic / rubber** | Seals, hose coverings, HMI panel frame | Plastic/waste recycling (local classification) |
| **Electrical / electronic (WEEE)** | Siemens S7-1200 PLC, HMI, drives, cables | WEEE / e-waste legislation — licensed processor |
| **Copper / aluminium** | Cable, heater-element connections, fan housings | Metal recycling |
| **Water / cleaning waste** | Tank drain water, soapy wash water, oily process water | **Section 10.1.8** — wastewater treatment/disposal; do not pour into domestic sewer |
| **Grease / oil** | Castrol Tribol GR 100-1 PD, seal leakage | Licensed oily waste |
| **Packaging** | Container, timber, stretch wrap, foam | Packaging recycling (local) |
| **Filter / consumable** | Pre-filter, bag filter (see **9.1.6**) | Wastewater or solid-waste class depending on soiling |

**Note:** For scrap evaluation the **Section 13.3.1** parts list may be used as a reference; for electronic parts the serial number (**1726050**) must be kept in the disposal record.
