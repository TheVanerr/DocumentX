# 12. DISMANTLING

This section defines the safe dismantling, temporary/permanent decommissioning and scrap/recycling procedures for the **KNV 30 3000 2B** machine. Dismantling must be performed only by authorized maintenance and electrical personnel.

Before dismantling, stop the machine, drain the tanks and apply the **LOTO procedure** (see **Section 2.4** — steps are not repeated). Liquid drain and tank cleaning are performed according to **Section 10.1.5**. Mechanical handling follows **Section 4.1** (forklift; crane prohibited).

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Hazardous substances (oil / battery / chemical) | **No** battery. Oily process water, grease, detergent and WEEE waste — **Sections 10.1.8** / **12.3** |
| Hydraulic system | Not present |
| Disposal | Environmental regulations of the country of use |
