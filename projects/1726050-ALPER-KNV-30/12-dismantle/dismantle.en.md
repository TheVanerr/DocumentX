# 12. DISMANTLING

Before dismantling, stop the machine, drain tanks and apply **LOTO** (see **9.1.2**, **10.1.2**).

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Hazardous substances | None |
| Disposal | Environmental regulations in country of use |
