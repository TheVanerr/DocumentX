# 2. SAFETY

The KNV 30 3000 2B industrial parts washing machine involves risks from hot process fluids, a moving conveyor, compressed air, electricity (380 V) and chemical exposure. This chapter defines the safety rules that must be followed during installation, commissioning, operation, maintenance and service of the machine. The instructions have been prepared in accordance with the principles of EN ISO 12100 and EN ISO 20607.

Violation of safety requirements creates the risk of injury, equipment damage, environmental impact and termination of warranty coverage. The organisation operating the machine (employer) is responsible for personnel assignment, training and accessibility of the manual (**See Chapter 1.1.3**).
